Evidence dial

What the readout can—and cannot—tell you

Strong signal

Same normalized message, tight timestamp spread, multiple stream identities, and one or two varying sharding labels.

Needs context

Recurring health messages or expected fan-out can look identical. Lens preserves evidence so an operator—not an algorithm—decides.

Next check

Compare retry intervals, automatic stream-sharding labels, and producer request IDs before changing the pipeline.
