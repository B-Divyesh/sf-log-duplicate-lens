Privacy

Effective August 28, 2026

The CLI reads logs on your machine. The browser workbench reads pasted or selected files in browser memory.

What is stored

The demo stores only an active marker under a demo: key. Resetting or leaving the demo removes it.

What is sent

The demo does not make an off-origin request. Log content is not included in page requests.

Analytics and third parties

This site uses no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising cookies, third-party fonts, or runtime content delivery networks.

Your choices

You may use the CLI without an account. The browser demo can run offline after the site has loaded. For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
