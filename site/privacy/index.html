Plain-language notice

Privacy

Effective August 28, 2026

Log Duplicate Lens is designed so log content stays under your control. The CLI runs entirely on your machine and contains no telemetry. The browser workbench analyzes pasted or selected files in browser memory; it does not upload, retain, or transmit log content.

What is stored

If you buy or restore Field Kit, the site stores the license token and its latest verification result in your browser’s local storage under keys beginning with sb_license:. Saved Field Kit presets are also stored locally. You can remove all of this by clearing site data.

What is sent

License verification sends only the license token to api.sociobot.in at most once per day. It does not send logs, presets, filenames, or report content. Checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, whose checkout privacy terms apply to purchase details.

Analytics and third parties

This version uses no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising cookies, third-party fonts, or runtime content delivery networks. The host may keep short-lived security and access logs needed to serve the site.

Your choices

You may use the free CLI without visiting the site or providing personal data. You may also use the browser analyzer offline after its shell has been cached. For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
